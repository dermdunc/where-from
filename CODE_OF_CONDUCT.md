# Code of conduct

This is a condensed adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

**The short version.** Be kind, be specific, and argue with the evidence rather than the person. Everyone is welcome here, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Not acceptable:** harassment of any kind; sexualised language or imagery; insults, trolling or personal attacks; publishing someone else's private information without their permission; anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Scope.** Issues, pull requests, and any other space where you're representing this project.

**Enforcement.** There is one maintainer. Report a problem privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile (@dermdunc) rather than in a public issue. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules, and will say why when it's safe to do so.
